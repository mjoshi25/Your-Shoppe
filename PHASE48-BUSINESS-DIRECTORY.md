# Phase 48 – Business directory

## Included
- Public approved business directory with keyword and city filters.
- Authenticated listing submission with business name/title, category, description, contact details, location, website and optional image URL.
- Listings enter PENDING status and are hidden from public search until approved.
- Owner listing-status page.
- Admin moderation: approve, reject or suspend.
- MongoDB collection: `community_listings` (type `BUSINESS`).

## Notes
This first release stores an optional image URL; it does not yet provide a direct Cloudinary upload widget, map/geocoding, ratings, enquiry inbox or paid promotion.
